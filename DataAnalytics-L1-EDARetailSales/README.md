# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Objective

Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to uncover sales trends, customer behavior patterns, product performance, and actionable business insights.

## Tech Stack

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed

- Dataset shape and structure inspection
- Data type and missing value analysis
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Quarterly sales trend analysis
- Customer segment distribution
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Revenue by product category
- Correlation heatmap
- Discount vs Profit analysis

## Key Insights

- Technology generated the highest total revenue.
- The Consumer segment had the highest number of orders.
- Staples were among the best-selling products by quantity.
- Sales and Profit showed a moderate positive relationship.
- Higher discount levels were associated with lower profitability and negative profit in several cases.

## Business Recommendations

1. Control excessive discounting to protect profit margins.
2. Invest in high-performing Technology products.
3. Focus marketing campaigns and loyalty programs on the Consumer segment.
4. Use seasonal sales trends for better inventory and marketing planning.
5. Keep high-demand products well-stocked and consider bundled offers.
